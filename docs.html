Welcome to Family

Family is a self-custody wallet: an app that holds your crypto while giving only you the keys. Think of it as a piggy bank that lives in your pocket — nobody else can open it, not even us.

🎉 New here? Download the app, tap Create wallet, and you'll be set up in under a minute. No seed phrase required.

The three things to know

1. You own your keys. Your wallet is created on your device, and your private keys never leave it. Encrypted backups keep you safe if your phone goes missing.

2. Everything is human-readable. You'll see names like maya.eth instead of 0x7f3a…9c2e, and dollar amounts instead of wei. If something looks confusing, that's our bug — tell us.

3. Safety is automatic. Every transaction is simulated before you sign. If our checks spot something risky, you'll see a clear warning like this:

⚠️  Transaction Safe
This contract was deployed 2 hours ago
and has no verified source code.
We recommend canceling.

What's next

Head to Creating your wallet in the sidebar, or just download the app and follow the friendly blob. It knows the way.
